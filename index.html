COCONOVA INNOVATION

Turn Coconut Husk Into Something Valuable.

CocoNova is a compact and affordable coconut husk processing machine designed for small-scale coconut vendors, farmers, and rural communities.

Instead of throwing away or selling coconut husks at low prices, users can process them into useful Coir Fibre and Cocopeat.
